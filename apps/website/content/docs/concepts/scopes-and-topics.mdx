## Topics (horizontal) — available

A topic (`ai`, `health`, …) is global, registered in `anatomy/topics.md`, and can span pillars and scopes. Create with [`mechanic-forge-topic`](/docs/use-cases/add-a-topic) or seed at install with `--seed-topics`.

## Scopes (vertical) — Planned

A scope is a context: an employer, a venture, personal life. Scopes are **optional**; v1 starts unscoped (root pillars only, empty `anatomy/scopes.md`).

- Per scope, in `anatomy/scopes.md`: `layout: folder` (content under `scopes/<scope>/`) or `layout: tag` (root pillars + frontmatter `scope`); `private`; storage `reference` (summary + link to source of truth) or `copy`; bound accounts.
- Person-level data (`me`, `network`, `pulse`, `journey`) always stays at root; people/companies/tasks carry `scope: [...]`.
- Wikilinks cross scopes freely; a `private` scope is excluded from other scopes' context unless explicitly linked.
- Processes accept `scope=` and `topic=` filters; export/archive per scope.

Scope mechanics (`mechanic-forge-scope`, `mechanic-scope-move`) and the path resolver: **Planned**, [#76](https://github.com/rucka/gilberto/issues/76).
